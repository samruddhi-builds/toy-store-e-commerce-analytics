# Toy Store E-Commerce Analytics

An end-to-end e-commerce analytics project built using Microsoft Excel and Power Query to transform multi-table transactional data into business-focused insights and an interactive dashboard.

## 📊 Project Overview

This project analyzes an e-commerce dataset from Maven Analytics to understand product performance, revenue, profitability, refunds, marketing performance, and yearly trends.

The analysis follows the business flow:

**Traffic → Sessions → Orders → Products → Revenue → Gross Profit → Refunds**

## 🛠️ Tools & Techniques

- **Microsoft Excel**
- **Power Query**
  - Data extraction
  - Data cleaning
  - Data transformation
  - Table merging
  - Data modeling
- **PivotTables**
- **PivotCharts**
- **Interactive Slicers**
- **Dashboard Design**

## 🔍 Business Questions

The analysis focuses on:

1. Which products generate the highest sales and gross profit?
2. How much revenue and gross profit are generated?
3. How do refunds affect product performance?
4. Which marketing campaigns and content are associated with higher revenue?
5. How does performance change across years?

## 📈 Dashboard

The interactive Excel dashboard includes:

- Total Revenue
- Total Gross Profit
- Total Refund Amount
- Total Items Sold
- Total Orders
- Gross Profit by Product
- Items Sold by Product
- Revenue by Marketing Campaign
- Revenue by Marketing Content
- Gross Profit by Year
- Refund Amount by Product
- Interactive slicers for filtering the analysis

## 💡 Key Insights

- **The Original Mr. Fuzzy** recorded the highest gross profit and item sales among the products analyzed.
- **Nonbrand** was the marketing campaign associated with the highest revenue.
- **g_ad_3** was the content variation associated with the highest revenue.
- **The Original Mr. Fuzzy** also recorded the highest total refund amount, making refunds an important area to monitor alongside its strong sales performance.
- Gross profit varied across the years analyzed, with **2014 recording the highest gross profit**.
